- Child sessions are owned through the actual parent route, with same browser context and distinct global session/command IDs. Global uniqueness is stricter bounded bookkeeping than reused numerical IDs; foreign equal-ID replies remain refused.
- A child attached before discovery keeps its actual attachment origin; later discovery reconciles it. An actual attachment's own target information is retained immediately before its route record.
