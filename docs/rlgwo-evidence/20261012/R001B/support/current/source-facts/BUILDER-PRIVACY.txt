  <p class="note"><strong>Self-configuring embeds &amp; privacy:</strong> location is approximate and uses <a href="https://www.geojs.io/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">GeoJS</a>, with ipinfo.io as fallback. Place/postcode searches go to Nominatim and, for matching Canadian-style prefixes, Zippopotam. Builder reverse lookups send coordinates to BigDataCloud; the widget also requests prayer, weather, radar and font resources. Saved settings stay in your browser, which does not prevent these service requests. Precise location is optional and requires browser/host permission. An iframe host, including TablissNG, can strip <code>allow="geolocation"</code>; coarse/manual setup remain available. Storage policies can limit persistence. <a href="https://github.com/theislampill/salah_widget#auto-detect-precise-location--privacy" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">Request details</a>.</p>
