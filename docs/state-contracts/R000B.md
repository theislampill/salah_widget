## Authority and explicit consumer states

`weatherEligibility()` and `weatherDecision()` remain the existing authority. Both source and receipt ages are inclusive from 0 to 900,000 ms; future allowance remains zero. A publication receipt records what the cloud consumer used. It does not introduce a new freshness clock, admission rule, provider request, prayer model or observation claim.

| Cloud publication state | Meaning | Allowed next transition |
|---|---|---|
| `uninitialised` | No complete atmosphere has published a cloud deck in this consumer lifetime. | A complete eligible atmosphere establishes a deck; otherwise the consumer remains empty. |
| `eligible-published` | Coverage is bound to the actual admitted record, target generation and mode used by a complete atmosphere. | Retain while its own consumed authority is current under the exact-record no-model rule or the bounded healthy full-model handoff below; refresh through a complete atmosphere; withdraw when either retention rule fails. |
| `withdrawn-unavailable` | No cloud coverage, readiness, density or backing raster is authorized for this publication. | A genuinely current complete atmosphere may restore coverage. Fresh weather alone cannot revive old cloud material. |
| `superseded` | A scene/target replacement revoked the old publication. | The new owner must publish from its own current evidence. Returning to the same coordinates does not revive the old generation. |

The receipt contains a revision, state, reason, target, lane, source-valid time, retrieval time, condition state and consumption time. The private record association also binds the consumed scene and mode; it is not a second public mutable eligibility flag. Raw records and cache provenance can remain after withdrawal.

### Policy when the prayer timetable is unavailable

A warm deck may remain while its **own exact consumed record and target, plus every independently consumed present-evidence identity and lease,** remain current. A stable model record does not renew or substitute for that separate evidence authority. A cold or already withdrawn consumer stays empty. An admitted replacement cannot lend fresh timestamps to an older deck; without a complete atmosphere it is withheld until ordinary prayer recovery permits a real complete publication.

The weather header remains independently usable. Model-estimated weather stays labelled as such. Missing or expired weather is unavailable, not measured clear or dry. Physical particles and optional weather optics that require the complete atmosphere are withheld in the no-timetable path. Independently current celestial bodies and catalogue output remain useful; no synthetic prayer data is manufactured to unlock them.

### Healthy complete-model refresh handoff

A newly admitted record B can arrive before the next ordinary complete render consumes it. During that gap, a previously published A deck may remain only while its existing receipt is still active, both records are real and eligible, the target generation, lane, scene and mode still match, and the actual prayer `model()` remains available. The per-record `selectedWeather(now, record)` selector applies A's own existing live or explicit-preview rule. B cannot lend A its source or retrieval time. Any direct present evidence consumed by A must remain the same independently eligible evidence. This guard applies before the exact-record shortcut as well as during a different-record healthy handoff; retaining the same model record alone is insufficient.

This is retention of the original A receipt and its already published state. It does not create a deadline, renew a timestamp, publish B, call a producer, or take an extra easing step. The ordinary complete render consumes B once using the unchanged easing gain. A genuine SIM or forecast object replacement follows its existing preview rule without invented live metadata. Cold and previously withdrawn consumers gain no restoration path, and a replacement with no actual prayer model remains withheld as described above.

Reconciliation before painter/capture and the pure final-publication check use the same authority predicate. The final check additionally requires the exact active receipt identity: A's pending decode cannot publish after B has completed its own atmosphere publication, after withdrawal, or after a target, scene or mode invalidation. Repeated captures alone do not change coverage, readiness, density, raster, population, phases or consumed-effect provenance.

A genuine complete publication receives a new receipt when its consumed present-evidence identity, render code or condition state changes, even if the selected model record is unchanged. Repeated publication of the same still-current evidence and semantics remains idempotent. The new receipt records the decision actually consumed; a pure final read cannot adopt the replacement or revive the prior receipt. A valid complete-model dry replacement may continue normal easing toward its new targets. That supported clear/dry state remains distinct from unavailable weather, which cannot be relabelled as measured clear.
