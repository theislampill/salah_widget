## Decoding and interpretation

`decodePublishDecision(record, ownerEpoch)` is a pure schema/epoch decoder,
not artifact authentication, a complete-history validator or scientific
admission. It requires the exact first-false prefix: evaluated masks are
1/3/7/15/31/63/127 and equal bits are all preceding bits. The event epoch must
equal `previousEpoch + 1`. Missing, impossible, extra or unbound records refuse.

The decoder reports `EQUAL`, `DIFFERENT`, or **NOT_EVALUATED** per term. It never
evaluates a skipped comparison later. Its kind is precisely
`SOURCE_ABSENT_AT_REPLACEMENT` or `SOURCE_PRESENT_AT_REPLACEMENT`, describing only
the current source at the later replacement boundary. Absence can follow first
acquisition, revocation or reentrant clearing; it does not identify historical
ownership or source presence at earlier comparisons. `preparingEpoch` shows the existing slot;
`latestQueued` shows the existing queue flag. Neither assigns elapsed cost or
proves that queueing dominated a gap. No CPU clock is observed.

The comparison prefix and `hadSource` are observed at different moments. A
getter may revoke the current owner during a later comparison, leaving earlier
comparisons equal and `hadSource` false at the replacement boundary. The decoder
must preserve both observations without using the later flag to infer an earlier
comparison result. The focused clear-during-cloud-comparison control covers this
case; the initial source leaf's stricter inference was rejected in independent
review and its failing evidence remains retained.

Reentry after the snapshot, during the original source assignment, can advance
the epoch before the outer owner event. The observation leaves that original
behavior intact and refuses to bind the outer snapshot to a different epoch.
The dedicated late-reentry control verifies both behavior parity and this
refusal. Complete-history intake must mark that history incomplete;
it must not backfill a snapshot, reassign an epoch, or loosen event binding.
