## 1. Implemented in this candidate (C1)

### 1.1 Phase-direction authority

`src/native/index.html` `nativeLunarPhaseDirection` sits beside `SalahMoonHost.capture`, outside the accepted
V5/CP9 material section, which stays byte-identical. For the minute snapshot that `moonSky` holds, it gives:

- +1 while α = acos(2f−1) falls over the next minute;
- −1 while it rises;
- 0 in a 1e−6 rad/min dead band.

It is distinct from `waxing` (the lit limb, χ sign), which is unchanged. It is absent for `simMoon` overrides and
without a location.

The directed scene is built from the unchanged `nativeCalendarMoonScene` (`moon/src/moon-precision.mjs`,
byte-identical to main) with the lead's sense, and then the lit side is restored from `waxing`:

- in `moon/src/moon-native.mjs` `directedCalendarScene`;
- for the head in `moon/src/moon-first-paint.mjs` `headDirectedScene`, which takes its direction from the owner's own
  snapshot one minute apart.

The scene records `leadDirection`. `phaseDrift` signs by the record's own lead, and both re-request skips follow
through `phaseDrift`. The capture validates `phaseDirection`.

Outside the census intervals the refinement scene is identical to main: 4306 of 4320 ten-minute samples over 30 days
are identical, and the 14 that differ all fall inside the 24 Nov and 8 Dec 2026 intervals
(`tools/qa/r0041/scene-identity.cjs`). Refined and initial V5 bytes are therefore unchanged there by construction.
The R0022 baseline guards pass unamended.

### 1.2 One presenter: the runtime-owned detail handle in `moon/src/moon-native.mjs`

- **Same-scope expiry:**
  1. The record's authority is withdrawn.
  2. The successor tier is adopted in the same task.
  3. The historical withdrawal runs only when no successor is adopted.
- **`clear()` routes** (direct `invalidate`, profile or reference changes), when the identity is unchanged: same
  scope, continuous clock, visible and unpaused. The successor is adopted before anything presented is withdrawn, and
  the host is then notified as before. A genuine identity, clock or visibility change keeps the truthful withdrawal.
- **Every initial adoption** requests the refined-sky compose in the same task.
- **Texture replacement** (`SalahMoonHost.publish`): the presented `.mphoto` texture is kept until its successor image
  is decoded. The first texture is set at once.

### 1.3 Resume: compose on resume (owner decision 1)

- **Hide is unchanged.** ISM-MOON-019 and its accepted `host.test.cjs` case: on hide the runtime withdraws
  everything (`clear('document hidden')`, host fallback, detail cleared). The pre-hide surface is never retained.
- **Mechanism of the old blank** (in-page probe, Chromium): the Moon runtime's `visibilitychange` listener runs
  before the native host removes its own `.c.paused` class, so its poll still reads `paused` and adopts nothing.
  The preview host then republishes in the same task (unchanged decoded mask). Its `real-sky-preview-ready` class hides
  the SVG Moon while the detail compose finds no surface. The slot stayed empty until the 4 Hz poll adopted the
  successor (about 100 ms) and the next preview paint composed it (about 35 ms more).
- **Correction (one presenter, no new writer).**
  - The runtime-owned handle's `compose()` first calls `presentForCompose()`. When nothing is presentable, this
    runs the same `poll()` the 4 Hz timer runs, so the current successor (the initial V5 tier for the current scene)
    is adopted before the host's compose joins it. A re-entrant refined-sky compose is suppressed during that call.
    In a steady presented state it does nothing (R41-RES-4).
  - For a bare host (no sky frame published yet), the runtime's visible branch registers one animation-frame
    callback. It runs before the next paint and presents the successor through the same function.
  - `SalahMoonHost.publish` keeps decode-before-swap only while the texture is presented (`moon-ready`). A withdrawn
    texture is replaced at once, so the pre-hide `.mphoto` image is never revealed by a later adoption.
  - While the atmospheric preview owns the sky (its ready class hides the SVG Moon and no refined frame exists), an
    adoption asks the preview host to re-compose in the same task (`107e251b`, R41-RES-5). Without this, the resume
    change let the preview's sky show through the slot for one paint after a genuine identity change. That was
    witnessed in all three engines and is 0 after the fix.
- **Tests:** `moon/tests/r0041-direction.test.cjs` R41-RES-1, -2 and -3 are RED on `fccee193` and GREEN here;
  R41-RES-4 is a steady-state guard.
- **Back/forward cache.** No engine restores from the bfcache under Playwright. Chromium reports
  `BackForwardCacheDisabledForDelegate` even without `--disable-back-forward-cache`; Firefox and WebKit reload the
  page. The witness therefore also runs `pageshow`: the bfcache lifecycle synthesised in spec order (`pagehide`
  persisted, hidden; 1 s; visible, `pageshow` persisted). On that event the asset owner suspends and resumes as on a
  real restore. **A real bfcache restore is not witnessed.**

### 1.4 Expiry with no successor: the calendar-disc fallback (owner decision 2)

The V5 calendar presentation (`moon-calendar.mjs` `calendarProxy`, `blendCalendarBytes`) is derived from a rendered
phase surface. It has exactly two producers: the V5 initial receiver (`window.SalahMoonInitial.render`) and a validated
worker result (R41-CAL-SRC asserts this statically). It is not an independent renderer. The legacy
`renderMoonCalendarFallback` is the dormant deprecated-legacy path (R0033 DG-1 = B, never revealed) and is not used.

| Condition | Calendar presentation available for the current phase? | Candidate behaviour | Route |
|---|---|---|---|
| Receiver present and eligible | **Yes**: `initialPresentation` renders the V5 tier for the current capture | Adopted in the same task and composed through the one presenter, with no withdrawal first. Below the horizon (`up = 0`) it is the calendar disc the product already shows (`calendarProxyWeight` 1); above, the lit V5 tier. Phase is within the unchanged bound. The scene is identical to the refined job for the same capture (terminator, lit limb, footprint) (R41-CAL-1) | **MET** (VM; native `expiry`, §2) |
| Receiver missing (absent at construction, or removed) | **No**: the only producers are the absent receiver and the in-flight worker job (asynchronous) | Truthful withdrawal (host fallback, detail cleared) until the refined result arrives (R41-CAL-2a; native `noSucc`) | **NOT MET** |
| Receiver decode failure (`SalahMoonInitialError`, R0033 `permanent`) | **No**, for the same reason | Same (R41-CAL-2b) | **NOT MET** |
| R0033 initial-tier budget exhausted (three render throws) | **No**: calling the receiver again would break R0033 M2's accepted budget (not authorised) | Same; the receiver is not called (R41-CAL-3; native `noSuccThrow`) | **NOT MET** |
| R0033 60 s re-attempt wait (one or two throws) | Not until the wait ends (R0033 M2) | Same (R41-CAL-4); the V5 tier returns at its next eligibility | **NOT MET inside the wait** |

- **Moonlight eligibility** stays bound to the presented surface (R0033 M4: `presentable` drives
  `moonSurfacePresentable`).
  - **When the fallback is available**, the V5 tier IS a presented physical surface. The one presence predicate is
    true, and the existing native rules decide light. The R0022 below-horizon, near-new and daylight reasons still
    zero it, so the calendar disc below the horizon grants no moonlight.
  - **When it is not available**, nothing is presented, so light is withdrawn with the surface
    (`surface-unavailable`), exactly as R0033 certified.
  - **The fallback itself** never grants light and never withdraws it beyond what the presented surface implies
    (R41-CAL-LIGHT). This is truthful because light follows exactly what is painted in the slot.
- **No second presenter and no legacy Moon.** Everything goes through the runtime's `adopt()` and the runtime-owned
  handle; the census is unchanged.
- **R0033 boundary.**
  - *Construction failure* (DG-1 = B) means that the `moon-native` construction threw. No runtime and no presenter
    handle are registered (`SalahMoonRuntime` and `SalahMoonDetail` are both null), and `SalahMoonConstructionError`
    is set. This is preserved exactly: R41-CAL-R33, beside the unamended R33-13 and R33-14.
  - *Expiry with no successor* requires a registered runtime that holds an accepted refined record, so it can never
    arise under construction failure.
  - A missing or failed receiver inside a registered runtime is **not** construction failure and is not covered by
    the DG-1 = B exception. It is reported above as NOT MET, not reclassified.
- **Hand-off when the refined result arrives.** It is adopted in place, with no `detail.clear()` and no host fallback
  (R41-CAL-HANDOFF). The visible change is measured natively (§2).

## 2. Native qualification (final head `107e251b`)

### 2.1 Method

The pixel witness (`tools/qa/r0041/witness.cjs`) classifies painted pixels inside the disc mask.

- **Capture.**
  - Chromium: the DevTools screencast, with in-page event times.
  - Firefox and WebKit: a serial screenshot loop. Each shot is attributed to its *request* instant, and each event to
    this process's clock when the forcing call returned.
  - Firefox and WebKit take a dedicated reference shot after settling, because their serial loop can hold a single
    load-time shot across the whole settle.
- **Absence rule.** A frame is ABSENT when:
  - dRef > 4 against the reference; or
  - it has at least 10 point-like pixels above the run's own baseline. The baseline is needed because the Firefox
    DPR 2 relief has 46 such pixels in frames identical to the reference.
- **Per event.** The segment runs from the forcing call to 30 ms before the next forcing call. "With absence" means at
  least one ABSENT frame in that segment.
- **Identity routes** are judged at the Moon's end-of-run rectangle, because the Moon moves with the identity.
- **Other routes:** `natural` uses a rolling reference; `startup` counts absences after the first present frame;
  `noSucc` counts absent frames after settle.
- **Rejected harness readings.** Three earlier readings came from the harness, not the product, and are superseded:
  - Firefox and WebKit "blank for 4 s": a stale reference shot.
  - Firefox DPR 2 "stars": relief pixels.
  - Firefox and WebKit offsets: `performance.timeOrigin` differs from the shot clock by up to about 1 s.
- **Isolation.** Every launch went through `iso.cjs`. Allowed non-loopback requests: **0** in every run.
- **Fixed settings.** WebKit here is Playwright's WebKit build, **not Safari**. Night of 2026-11-18 16:30 Z (gibbous),
  except the census interval (2026-11-24 16:30 Z) and the C2 identity route (2026-11-24 15:30 Z, + 1 h).
- **Evidence:** `evidence/R0041/qualification/final-107e251b-aggregate.json` and
  `main-06b6e93b-and-c2-aggregate.json`. Recipes: `tools/qa/r0041/run-cells.sh`, `summarise.py`, `aggregate.py`.

first record, because it derives the direction from the ephemeris one minute ahead.

## 3. Open routes and the decisions they need

- **Expiry with no successor, receiver missing / decode-failed / R0033 budget exhausted or waiting (NOT MET).** The
  owner's calendar-disc fallback is unavailable in exactly these conditions, because the calendar presentation's only
  synchronous producer is the receiver that is missing or ineligible (§1.4, R41-CAL-2a/2b/3/4). Possible next
  directions, none authorised:
  - (a) a receiver-independent calendar renderer, which would be a new producer;
  - (b) allowing the R0033 budget one calendar render per expiry, which changes R0033 M2;
  - (c) refining ahead of expiry, which changes the refinement scheduling.

  This needs an owner decision.
- **WebKit resume and `pageshow` first shot (PARTLY).** Settling it needs a compositor-level WebKit capture or a real
  recording; the screenshot loop cannot.
- **Real bfcache restore (NOT DONE).** It cannot be driven under Playwright in any engine (§1.3).
