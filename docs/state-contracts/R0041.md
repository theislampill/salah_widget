## 1. Implemented in this candidate (C1)

### 1.1 Phase-direction authority

`src/native/index.html` `nativeLunarPhaseDirection` sits beside `SalahMoonHost.capture`, outside the accepted
V5/CP9 material section, which stays byte-identical. For the minute snapshot that `moonSky` holds, it gives:

- +1 while α = acos(2f−1) falls over the next minute;
- −1 while it rises;
- 0 in a 1e−6 rad/min dead band.

It is distinct from `waxing` (the lit limb, χ sign), which is unchanged. It is absent for `simMoon` overrides and
without a location.

The directed scene is built from the unchanged `nativeCalendarMoonScene` (`moon/src/moon-precision.mjs`,
byte-identical to main) with the lead's sense, and then the lit side is restored from `waxing`:

- in `moon/src/moon-native.mjs` `directedCalendarScene`;
- for the head in `moon/src/moon-first-paint.mjs` `headDirectedScene`, which takes its direction from the owner's own
  snapshot one minute apart.

The scene records `leadDirection`. `phaseDrift` signs by the record's own lead, and both re-request skips follow
through `phaseDrift`. The capture validates `phaseDirection`.

Outside the census intervals the refinement scene is identical to main: 4307 of 4320 ten-minute samples over 30 days
are identical, and the 13 that differ all fall inside the 24 Nov (15:58–17:36 Z) and 8 Dec (23:22–23:58 Z) 2026 intervals
(`tools/qa/r0041/scene-identity.cjs`). Refined and initial V5 bytes are therefore unchanged there by construction.
The R0022 baseline guards pass unamended.

### 1.2 One presenter: the runtime-owned detail handle in `moon/src/moon-native.mjs`

- **Same-scope expiry:**
  1. The record's authority is withdrawn.
  2. The successor tier is adopted in the same task.
  3. The historical withdrawal runs only when no successor is adopted.
- **`clear()` routes** (direct `invalidate`, profile or reference changes), when the identity is unchanged: same
  scope, continuous clock, visible and unpaused. The successor is adopted before anything presented is withdrawn, and
  the host is then notified as before. A genuine identity, clock or visibility change keeps the truthful withdrawal.
- **Every initial adoption** requests the refined-sky compose in the same task.
- **Texture replacement** (`SalahMoonHost.publish`): the presented `.mphoto` texture is kept until its successor image
  is decoded. The first texture is set at once.

### 1.3 Resume: compose on resume (owner decision 1)

- **Hide is unchanged.** ISM-MOON-019 and its accepted `host.test.cjs` case: on hide the runtime withdraws
  everything (`clear('document hidden')`, host fallback, detail cleared). The pre-hide surface is never retained.
- **Mechanism of the old blank** (in-page probe, Chromium): the Moon runtime's `visibilitychange` listener runs
  before the native host removes its own `.c.paused` class, so its poll still reads `paused` and adopts nothing.
  The preview host then republishes in the same task (unchanged decoded mask). Its `real-sky-preview-ready` class hides
  the SVG Moon while the detail compose finds no surface. The slot stayed empty until the 4 Hz poll adopted the
  successor (about 100 ms) and the next preview paint composed it (about 35 ms more).
- **Correction (one presenter, no new writer).**
  - The runtime-owned handle's `compose()` first calls `presentForCompose()`. When nothing is presentable, this
    runs the same `poll()` the 4 Hz timer runs, so the current successor (the initial V5 tier for the current scene)
    is adopted before the host's compose joins it. A re-entrant refined-sky compose is suppressed during that call.
    In a steady presented state it does nothing (R41-RES-4).
  - For a bare host (no sky frame published yet), the runtime's visible branch registers one animation-frame
    callback. It runs before the next paint and presents the successor through the same function.
  - `SalahMoonHost.publish` keeps decode-before-swap only while the texture is presented (`moon-ready`). A withdrawn
    texture is replaced at once, so the pre-hide `.mphoto` image is never revealed by a later adoption.
  - While the atmospheric preview owns the sky (its ready class hides the SVG Moon and no refined frame exists), an
    adoption asks the preview host to re-compose in the same task (`107e251b`, R41-RES-5). Without this, the resume
    change let the preview's sky show through the slot for one paint after a genuine identity change. That was
    witnessed in all three engines and is 0 after the fix.
- **Tests:** `moon/tests/r0041-direction.test.cjs` R41-RES-1, -2 and -3 are RED on `fccee193` and GREEN here;
  R41-RES-4 is a steady-state guard.
- **Back/forward cache.** No engine restores from the bfcache under Playwright. Chromium reports
  `BackForwardCacheDisabledForDelegate` even without `--disable-back-forward-cache`; Firefox and WebKit reload the
  page. The witness therefore also runs `pageshow`: the bfcache lifecycle synthesised in spec order (`pagehide`
  persisted, hidden; 1 s; visible, `pageshow` persisted). On that event the asset owner suspends and resumes as on a
  real restore. **A real bfcache restore is not witnessed.**

### 1.4 Expiry with no successor: the calendar-disc fallback (owner decision 2)

The V5 calendar presentation (`moon-calendar.mjs` `calendarProxy`, `blendCalendarBytes`) is derived from a rendered
phase surface. It has exactly two producers: the V5 initial receiver (`window.SalahMoonInitial.render`) and a validated
worker result (R41-CAL-SRC asserts this statically). It is not an independent renderer. The legacy
`renderMoonCalendarFallback` is the dormant deprecated-legacy path (R0033 DG-1 = B, never revealed) and is not used.

| Receiver present and eligible | **Yes**: `initialPresentation` renders the V5 tier for the current capture | Adopted in the same task and composed through the one presenter, with no withdrawal first. The scene is identical to the refined job for the same capture (terminator, lit limb, footprint) (R41-CAL-1) | **MET** (VM; native `expiry`, §2) |

| Receiver decode failure (`SalahMoonInitialError`, R0033 `permanent`) | **No**, for the same reason | Same (R41-CAL-2b) | **NOT MET** |
| R0033 initial-tier budget exhausted (three render throws) | **No**: calling the receiver again would break R0033 M2's accepted budget (not authorised) | Same; the receiver is not called (R41-CAL-3; native `noSuccThrow`) | **NOT MET** |
| R0033 60 s re-attempt wait (one or two throws) | Not until the wait ends (R0033 M2) | Same (R41-CAL-4); the V5 tier returns at its next eligibility | **NOT MET inside the wait** |

- **Moonlight eligibility** stays bound to the presented surface (R0033 M4: `presentable` drives
  `moonSurfacePresentable`).
  - **When the fallback is available**, the V5 tier IS a presented physical surface. The one presence predicate is
    true, and the existing native rules decide light. The R0022 below-horizon, near-new and daylight reasons still
    zero it, so the calendar disc below the horizon grants no moonlight.
  - **When it is not available**, nothing is presented, so light is withdrawn with the surface
    (`surface-unavailable`), exactly as R0033 certified.
  - **The fallback itself** never grants light and never withdraws it beyond what the presented surface implies
    (R41-CAL-LIGHT). This is truthful because light follows exactly what is painted in the slot.
- **No second presenter and no legacy Moon.** Everything goes through the runtime's `adopt()` and the runtime-owned
  handle; the census is unchanged.
- **R0033 boundary.**
  - *Construction failure* (DG-1 = B) means that the `moon-native` construction threw. No runtime and no presenter
    handle are registered (`SalahMoonRuntime` and `SalahMoonDetail` are both null), and `SalahMoonConstructionError`
    is set. This is preserved exactly: R41-CAL-R33, beside the unamended R33-13 and R33-14.
  - *Expiry with no successor* requires a registered runtime that holds an accepted refined record, so it can never
    arise under construction failure.
  - A missing or failed receiver inside a registered runtime is **not** construction failure and is not covered by
    the DG-1 = B exception. It is reported above as NOT MET, not reclassified.
- **Hand-off when the refined result arrives.** It is adopted in place, with no `detail.clear()` and no host fallback
  (R41-CAL-HANDOFF). The visible change is measured natively (§2).

receiver-independent phase disc of §6. The table and the R41-CAL-2a/2b/3/4 cases stay as the record of why the V5

## 2. Native qualification (final head `107e251b`)

### 2.1 Method

The pixel witness (`tools/qa/r0041/witness.cjs`) classifies painted pixels inside the disc mask.

- **Capture.**
  - Chromium: the DevTools screencast, with in-page event times.
  - Firefox and WebKit: a serial screenshot loop. Each shot is attributed to its *request* instant, and each event to
    this process's clock when the forcing call returned.
  - Firefox and WebKit take a dedicated reference shot after settling, because their serial loop can hold a single
    load-time shot across the whole settle.
- **Absence rule.** A frame is ABSENT when:
  - dRef > 4 against the reference; or
  - it has at least 10 point-like pixels above the run's own baseline. The baseline is needed because the Firefox
    DPR 2 relief has 46 such pixels in frames identical to the reference.
- **Per event.** The segment runs from the forcing call to 30 ms before the next forcing call. "With absence" means at
  least one ABSENT frame in that segment.
- **Identity routes** are judged at the Moon's end-of-run rectangle, because the Moon moves with the identity.
- **Other routes:** `natural` uses a rolling reference; `startup` counts absences after the first present frame;
  `noSucc` counts absent frames after settle.
- **Rejected harness readings.** Three earlier readings came from the harness, not the product, and are superseded:
  - Firefox and WebKit "blank for 4 s": a stale reference shot.
  - Firefox DPR 2 "stars": relief pixels.
  - Firefox and WebKit offsets: `performance.timeOrigin` differs from the shot clock by up to about 1 s.
- **Isolation.** Every launch went through `iso.cjs`. Allowed non-loopback requests: **0** in every run.
- **Fixed settings.** WebKit here is Playwright's WebKit build, **not Safari**. Night of 2026-11-18 16:30 Z (gibbous),
  except the census interval (2026-11-24 16:30 Z) and the C2 identity route (2026-11-24 15:30 Z, + 1 h).
- **Evidence:** `evidence/R0041/qualification/final-107e251b-aggregate.json` and
  `main-06b6e93b-and-c2-aggregate.json`. Recipes: `tools/qa/r0041/run-cells.sh`, `summarise.py`, `aggregate.py`.

## 5. Proposed registry text (prose only, effective on acceptance)

> **ISM-MOON-003 (R0041 rows).**
> - One phase-direction authority (`phaseDirection`, the actual motion of the phase angle) governs the lead, the
>   drift sign and the re-request skips; `waxing` remains the lit limb.
> - Same-identity withdrawals (expiry, direct invalidation) adopt and compose the successor before anything
>   presented is withdrawn; genuine identity changes keep the truthful withdrawal.
> - While the atmospheric preview owns the sky it composes an adopted successor in the same task.
> - Open: expiry with no producer of a current presentation (receiver missing, decode failure, R0033 initial budget

## 6. Independent Moon fallback: the phase disc (owner decision of 2026-11-17)

Candidate engineering and independent evaluation only. Nothing here is merged, accepted, or a change to an accepted
scientific contract. Seven points remain the owner's decisions (§6.8); the candidate is built on stated defaults for
them and does not treat them as settled.

### 6.1 What was built

- **A producer, not a presenter.** `moon/src/moon-phase-disc.mjs` `renderPhaseDisc(scene)` returns one message of the
  shape the runtime's `adopt()` already takes. It is the accepted V5 initial operator applied to a smooth sphere of the
  mean lunar radius with one uniform material: the same canonical camera, 16-sample finite Sun,
  Lommel-Seeliger/Lambert law, analytic Lambert Earth integral, V5 display mapping, calendar token and box coding.
  It reads no receiver bytes, terrain, albedo map, clock, ephemeris or document, and it has no retry state. It draws
  no lunar markings.
- **Uniform material.** `[0.413235, 0.393423, 0.384153]` (linear, before the material gain): the disc mean of the
  accepted receiver material over its 196,435 records (R41-PD-ALB recomputes it from
  `moon/assets/initial-receivers.bin`).
- **Wiring** (`moon/src/moon-native.mjs`, `initialPresentation`): when the receiver cannot serve the current scene
  the runtime renders the phase disc for the same `scene(s)`, builds the same record (`key`, `scope`, `epoch`),
  re-reads `matches(record, capture())` and adopts it through the same `adopt()`. The compose request after an
  adoption is one shared function for both producers.
- **Causes** (recoverable renderer unavailability; `state.phaseDisc.cause`): `receiver-missing`,
  `receiver-decode-failed`, `initial-exhausted`, `initial-waiting`, `initial-throw`.
- **Unchanged:** `clear()`, `poll()`, `presentForCompose()`, the resume listener, `failed()`, `syncTexture()`, the
  detail handle, `request()`, `surface()`, `presentable`, `observe()`, `SalahMoonHost`, `updateSkySurface`, every CSS
  rule, `moon-detail.mjs`, `moon-initial.mjs`, `moon-precision.mjs`, the head (`moonHeadInitial`), the worker. The
  receiver is still called in one place under the unchanged `initialEligible` (R0033 M2: 60 s, one attempt per
  eligibility event, three throws per page). `PHASE_POSITION_BUDGET` is `.0416`. No file under `v1/`, `vendor/` or
  `real-sky/` is authored-changed; no CSP line changed; the legacy renderer (ISM-MOON-001) is not called, revealed or
  copied.
- **Diagnostics** (`src/native/index.html`, `qaState().moonTruth` only): `surface.status` `phase-disc`, a texture and
  Earthshine description that say "uniform sphere … no lunar terrain or albedo" and name the cause, and
  `moonTruth.limitation`.
- **Build.** `tools/build_moon.py` `HOST_UNITS` gains the unit. `python tools/build_native.py` twice: identical
  outputs, inventory `bcea792a…`. Pins rebound with `tools/qa/r0041/rebind-pins.cjs` (the R0041 pair and the
  `moon-initial` split pin only; the R0036 and R0033 retained-input records are byte-identical to head `61bd9b89`) and

### 6.3 Hand-over (source reading; pixels in §6.5)

- **Normal → phase disc** (same-identity invalidation or expiry, receiver unusable): one task withdraws the record's
  authority, renders and adopts the phase disc, replaces the vector texture at once (a different record) and
  composes. The detail canvas and the reveal classes are never cleared in between.
- **Phase disc → normal:** a worker preview or result is adopted in place (the phase-disc record is provisional, like
  the initial tier, so refinement is still requested); the receiver replaces it in place at its next R0033 eligibility;
  a genuine identity, clock or visibility change keeps the truthful withdrawal.
- **Resume, back/forward:** hide still withdraws everything (ISM-MOON-019). The existing resume path ends in the phase
  disc when the receiver is unusable.

### 6.5 Native witness (`tools/qa/r0041/phase-disc-witness.cjs`)

**Method.** Playwright, one browser job at a time, every launch through `iso.cjs` (loopback only; allowed
non-loopback requests **0** in every cell and in the control). Synthetic fixture (lat 24.47, lon 39.61), night of
2026-11-18 16:30 Z (gibbous, above the horizon), **real speed**, reduced motion. "WebKit" is Playwright's WebKit build,
not Safari. Two page loads per run:

- **Load A** — the embedded receiver does not decode (start-up failure) and the worker assets are withheld (404), so
  the phase disc is the only presentation: start-up; `invalidate()` ×3; hide 1 s / show ×3; the back/forward
  lifecycle synthesised in spec order ×3; the opacity triple; then the assets are restored, the worker is retried and
  the refined result replaces the phase disc; one more invalidation (refined → phase disc → refined).
- **Load B** — healthy start, settled refined Moon: the receiver is removed, `invalidate()` ×3 (missing receiver);
  then the receiver throws on every render, `invalidate()` ×3 spends the R0033 budget and ×3 more follow (exhausted).

**Pixel-side judgement.** A frame is ABSENT when its disc region is more than 4 codes (mean |ΔY|) from both
references (phase disc, refined) or holds at least 10 star-like pixels; the classifier arithmetic is `witness.cjs`'s.
State flags are auxiliary. Chromium at pixel ratio 1 is judged on the DevTools screencast with a one-pixel marker
recoloured on every animation frame for 3 s after each forcing call, so every compositor frame is delivered (about
60 per second); Firefox, WebKit and Chromium at ratio 2 are judged on serial device-scale screenshots (the screencast
can arrive at CSS size at ratio 2). A second marker is recoloured in the same task as each forcing call, and a frame
belongs to an event only when it shows that event's marker. Start-up is judged from the first frame in which a sky
control patch shows stars; frames before it are the neutral pending card.

**Fail closed.** A load is INVALID, never a pass, when: the fault was not armed (page state before the first event);
no animation frame within 3 s of a forcing call reports the phase disc as the current source with a higher render
count; the disc region is empty or partial; a judged frame has no reference comparison; the phase-disc reference is
not a distinct presented Moon; a hidden interval or the deliberate hide is not classified absent; no star lies behind
the disc in the hidden shot; a segment has fewer than 20 frames (screencast) or 5 (screenshots); or the receiver was
called more than three times. Three earlier attempts were discarded and re-run, and their verdicts were not used: a first Chromium ratio-1 round
(the exercise rule compared animation-frame timestamps instead of record order, and the first sky-control patch
counted the header buckle of the pending card as stars); Firefox (the animation-frame log was truncated at 30,000
records); Chromium ratio 2 (screencast frames could not be compared with a device-scale reference).

### 6.6 Visual consequence and whole-scene coherence

- **What a viewer sees in a fault state.** The Moon stays where it is, at its size, upright, with the correct phase
  and lit side, opaque, at the same overall brightness (step about a third of a code). It is a smooth, evenly toned
  globe with limb darkening and a soft terminator. **Maria, craters and terminator relief are absent**, and the dark
  side carries Earthshine but no texture.
- **At a transfer** the surface markings disappear or reappear in one frame: 8.2 codes mean per disc pixel, up to
  51–59 codes, with about a fifth of the disc changing by more than 16 codes at this phase. For comparison the
  accepted initial → refined swap is 0.68–0.87 codes mean (§2.2). This is a visible change of texture; it is not a
  blank, a change of shape or position, or a change of brightness. It occurs only when the start-up renderer is
  faulted (no natural trigger is on record; every case here is injected).
- **Light and the rest of the scene.** The light decision does not change at any transfer (the presence predicate
  stays true), so the sky lift, cloud light, beam, glow and lunar optics neither fade nor step. Without the phase
  disc the same faults withdrew the disc in one frame while its light faded over 1.1–1.4 s (R0033 finding F-1) and
  left stars in the slot; that incoherence is removed on these routes. Day scenes and healthy nights are unchanged
  by construction (the phase disc is reached only when the receiver cannot serve); the healthy-path cells of §2 were
  not re-run in this round.
- **Verdicts not given here.** Physical-fidelity and experiential-fidelity verdicts with transition clips (AGENTS)
  belong to the independent reviewers and the panel.
