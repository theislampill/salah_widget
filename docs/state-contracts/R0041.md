## 1. Implemented in this candidate (C1)

### 1.1 Phase-direction authority

`src/native/index.html` `nativeLunarPhaseDirection` sits beside `SalahMoonHost.capture`, outside the accepted
V5/CP9 material section, which stays byte-identical. For the minute snapshot that `moonSky` holds, it gives:

- +1 while α = acos(2f−1) falls over the next minute;
- −1 while it rises;
- 0 in a 1e−6 rad/min dead band.

It is distinct from `waxing` (the lit limb, χ sign), which is unchanged. It is absent for `simMoon` overrides and
without a location.

The directed scene is built from the unchanged `nativeCalendarMoonScene` (`moon/src/moon-precision.mjs`,
byte-identical to main) with the lead's sense, and then the lit side is restored from `waxing`:

- in `moon/src/moon-native.mjs` `directedCalendarScene`;
- for the head in `moon/src/moon-first-paint.mjs` `headDirectedScene`, which takes its direction from the owner's own
  snapshot one minute apart.

The scene records `leadDirection`. `phaseDrift` signs by the record's own lead, and both re-request skips follow
through `phaseDrift`. The capture validates `phaseDirection`.

Outside the census intervals the refinement scene is identical to main: 4307 of 4320 ten-minute samples over 30 days
are identical, and the 13 that differ all fall inside the 24 Nov (15:58–17:36 Z) and 8 Dec (23:22–23:58 Z) 2026 intervals
(`tools/qa/r0041/scene-identity.cjs`). Refined and initial V5 bytes are therefore unchanged there by construction.
The R0022 baseline guards pass unamended.

### 1.2 One presenter: the runtime-owned detail handle in `moon/src/moon-native.mjs`

- **Same-scope expiry:**
  1. The record's authority is withdrawn.
  2. The successor tier is adopted in the same task.
  3. The historical withdrawal runs only when no successor is adopted.
- **`clear()` routes** (direct `invalidate`, profile or reference changes), when the identity is unchanged: same
  scope, continuous clock, visible and unpaused. The successor is adopted before anything presented is withdrawn, and

- **Every initial adoption** requests the refined-sky compose in the same task.
- **Texture replacement** (`SalahMoonHost.publish`): the presented `.mphoto` texture is kept until its successor image

### 1.3 Resume: compose on resume (owner decision 1)

- **Hide is unchanged.** ISM-MOON-019 and its accepted `host.test.cjs` case: on hide the runtime withdraws
  everything (`clear('document hidden')`, host fallback, detail cleared). The pre-hide surface is never retained.
- **Mechanism of the old blank** (in-page probe, Chromium): the Moon runtime's `visibilitychange` listener runs
  before the native host removes its own `.c.paused` class, so its poll still reads `paused` and adopts nothing.
  The preview host then republishes in the same task (unchanged decoded mask). Its `real-sky-preview-ready` class hides
  the SVG Moon while the detail compose finds no surface. The slot stayed empty until the 4 Hz poll adopted the
  successor (about 100 ms) and the next preview paint composed it (about 35 ms more).
- **Correction (one presenter, no new writer).**
  - The runtime-owned handle's `compose()` first calls `presentForCompose()`. When nothing is presentable, this
    runs the same `poll()` the 4 Hz timer runs, so the current successor (the initial V5 tier for the current scene)
    is adopted before the host's compose joins it. A re-entrant refined-sky compose is suppressed during that call.
    In a steady presented state it does nothing (R41-RES-4).
  - For a bare host (no sky frame published yet), the runtime's visible branch registers one animation-frame
    callback. It runs before the next paint and presents the successor through the same function.
  - `SalahMoonHost.publish` keeps decode-before-swap only while the texture is presented (`moon-ready`). A withdrawn
    texture is replaced at once, so the pre-hide `.mphoto` image is never revealed by a later adoption.
  - While the atmospheric preview owns the sky (its ready class hides the SVG Moon and no refined frame exists), an
    adoption asks the preview host to re-compose in the same task (`107e251b`, R41-RES-5). Without this, the resume
    change let the preview's sky show through the slot for one paint after a genuine identity change. That was
    witnessed in all three engines and is 0 after the fix.
- **Tests:** `moon/tests/r0041-direction.test.cjs` R41-RES-1, -2 and -3 are RED on `fccee193` and GREEN here;
  R41-RES-4 is a steady-state guard.
- **Owner-gate round (G8, §7.1).** The task that clears the native pause now composes the Moon itself, so the frame
  callback above is only a backstop, and a real back/forward-cache restoration and a real tab switch are witnessed in
  Chromium (§7.5). The "Back/forward cache" bullet below describes the position before that round.
- **Back/forward cache.** No engine restores from the bfcache under Playwright. Chromium reports
  `BackForwardCacheDisabledForDelegate` even without `--disable-back-forward-cache`; Firefox and WebKit reload the
  page. The witness therefore also runs `pageshow`: the bfcache lifecycle synthesised in spec order (`pagehide`
  persisted, hidden; 1 s; visible, `pageshow` persisted). On that event the asset owner suspends and resumes as on a
  real restore. **A real bfcache restore is not witnessed.**

## 2. Native qualification (final head `107e251b`)

### 2.1 Method

The pixel witness (`tools/qa/r0041/witness.cjs`) classifies painted pixels inside the disc mask.

- **Capture.**
  - Chromium: the DevTools screencast, with in-page event times.
  - Firefox and WebKit: a serial screenshot loop. Each shot is attributed to its *request* instant, and each event to
    this process's clock when the forcing call returned.
  - Firefox and WebKit take a dedicated reference shot after settling, because their serial loop can hold a single
    load-time shot across the whole settle.
- **Absence rule.** A frame is ABSENT when:
  - dRef > 4 against the reference; or
  - it has at least 10 point-like pixels above the run's own baseline. The baseline is needed because the Firefox
    DPR 2 relief has 46 such pixels in frames identical to the reference.
- **Per event.** The segment runs from the forcing call to 30 ms before the next forcing call. "With absence" means at
  least one ABSENT frame in that segment.
- **Identity routes** are judged at the Moon's end-of-run rectangle, because the Moon moves with the identity.
- **Other routes:** `natural` uses a rolling reference; `startup` counts absences after the first present frame;
  `noSucc` counts absent frames after settle.
- **Rejected harness readings.** Three earlier readings came from the harness, not the product, and are superseded:
  - Firefox and WebKit "blank for 4 s": a stale reference shot.
  - Firefox DPR 2 "stars": relief pixels.
  - Firefox and WebKit offsets: `performance.timeOrigin` differs from the shot clock by up to about 1 s.
- **Isolation.** Every launch went through `iso.cjs`. Allowed non-loopback requests: **0** in every run.
- **Fixed settings.** WebKit here is Playwright's WebKit build, **not Safari**. Night of 2026-11-18 16:30 Z (gibbous),
  except the census interval (2026-11-24 16:30 Z) and the C2 identity route (2026-11-24 15:30 Z, + 1 h).
- **Evidence** (`evidence/R0041/qualification/`; regenerated in the correction round of 2026-11-22 from the raw
  witness logs, with the start instant in every key so the census interval and the ordinary-night control are separate
  cells):
  - `final-107e251b-aggregate.json` (69 cells), `main-06b6e93b-and-c2-aggregate.json` (32 cells),
    `remeasure-61bd9b89-aggregate.json` (9 cells).
  - `raw-logs.sha256.json`: the name, size and SHA-256 of each of the 77 raw logs (about 58 MB, not committed). The
    four Chromium ratio-2 re-measurement logs are committed under `raw/`.
  - The regenerated figures (runs, events, events with absence, absent frames) equal the previously committed ones in
    every cell; what changed is the key, the validity verdict and the cell verdict.
- **Validity.** `witness.cjs` asserts validity itself since `61bd9b89`. The `107e251b` logs predate that, so
  `summarise.py` applies the same fail-closed rules to their raw frame logs (no frame; a judged frame without a
  reference comparison; a hidden interval not classified absent). The disc size was not recorded then and is not
  checked. `aggregate.py` FAILS a cell with an invalid run, and FAILS (not "unchecked") a cell whose summary carries no
  validity verdict; it exits 1 when any cell fails.
- **Recipes:** `tools/qa/r0041/run-cells.sh`, `summarise.py`, `aggregate.py`.

### 6.3 Hand-over (source reading; pixels in §6.5)

- **Normal → phase disc** (same-identity invalidation or expiry, receiver unusable): one task withdraws the record's
  authority, renders and adopts the phase disc, replaces the vector texture at once (a different record) and
  composes. The detail canvas and the reveal classes are never cleared in between.
- **Phase disc → normal:** a worker preview or result is adopted in place (the phase-disc record is provisional, like
  the initial tier, so refinement is still requested); the receiver replaces it in place at its next R0033 eligibility;
  a genuine identity, clock or visibility change keeps the truthful withdrawal.
- **Resume, back/forward:** hide still withdraws everything (ISM-MOON-019). The existing resume path ends in the phase
  disc when the receiver is unusable.

### 6.5 Native witness (`tools/qa/r0041/phase-disc-witness.cjs`)

**Method.** Playwright, one browser job at a time, every launch through `iso.cjs` (loopback only; allowed

### 6.6 Visual consequence and whole-scene coherence

- **What a viewer sees in a fault state.** The Moon stays where it is, at its size, upright, with the correct phase
  and lit side, opaque. At crescent, gibbous and below the horizon the overall brightness is the same (step 0.2–0.4 codes) and

  evenly grey circle, 3.75 codes brighter than the real disc.** Below the horizon it is a dim ashen disc with the
  phase, close to the refined one. **Maria, craters and terminator relief are absent**, and the dark
  side carries Earthshine but no texture.
- **At a transfer** the surface markings disappear or reappear in one frame. The size of that step depends on the
  phase (§6.5, per-phase table): crescent 2.2 codes mean per disc pixel (3% of the disc over 16 codes); gibbous 8.2

  over 16). For comparison the
  accepted initial → refined swap is 0.68–0.87 codes mean (§2.2). This is a visible change of texture; it is not a
  blank or a change of shape or position, and except at full Moon it is not a change of overall brightness. It occurs only when the start-up renderer is
  faulted (no natural trigger is on record; every case here is injected).
- **Light and the rest of the scene.** The light decision does not change at any transfer (the presence predicate
  stays true), so the sky lift, cloud light, beam, glow and lunar optics neither fade nor step. Without the phase
  disc the same faults withdrew the disc in one frame while its light faded over 1.1–1.4 s (R0033 finding F-1) and
  left stars in the slot; that incoherence is removed on these routes. Day scenes and healthy nights are unchanged

  not re-run in this round.

### 7.5 G6: real-world witnesses (`tools/qa/r0041/real-witness.cjs`; product tree `c111cdf0`)

### 9.3 G4: the uniform material value

**What the default is.** The phase disc's material `[0.413235, 0.393423, 0.384153]` is the receiver's disc mean

**Measured** (the private G2 recordings' stills at device pixel ratio 1 and 2, and the committed full-Moon crops of
§6.5; disc pixels; linear = sRGB-decoded display value):

| Scene | Display-code mean, phase disc − refined | Linear mean, phase disc ÷ refined | Constant that would match the linear mean |
|---|---|---|---|
| Crescent | +0.38 to +0.44 | 1.002–1.007 | ≈ 0.99–1.00 |
| Gibbous | +0.33 to +0.44 | 0.984–0.987 | ≈ 1.014–1.019 |
| Full | +3.6 | 1.035–1.059 | ≈ 0.95–0.97 |
| Below the horizon | +0.22 to +0.25 | 1.004–1.007 | ≈ 0.99–1.00 |

- The offset is not a calibration error of the constant: it changes sign between gibbous and full in linear terms, so
  no single material value matches the refined mean at every phase. The gibbous display-code excess comes with a
  linear deficit (the concave display encoding of a textured surface lowers its code mean); the full-Moon excess
  reflects the dark maria near the disc centre, where the brightest, least limb-darkened pixels are.
- **What mean-matching would buy** (image-space simulation: the phase-disc pixels scaled in linear light, then
  re-encoded; an estimate, not a render): at full Moon a constant of 0.964 brings the mean step from +3.8 to +1.3 codes
  but leaves the mean |ΔY| at 16.06 (from 16.15) and the share over 16 codes at 49.7% (from 48.2%); the same constant
  takes gibbous from +0.17 to −1.5 codes and its mean |ΔY| from 8.24 to 8.48. The hand-over step is the surface
  markings appearing or disappearing, not the mean.
- Moonlight is not affected by the material (light follows the ephemeris and the presentation predicate, §7.2), so no
  choice of constant changes the sky or the light.

**Disposition put to the owner (G4).** G4's definition asks only which uniform material value the phase disc
uses; it does not require the hand-over step to be reduced, and the visible texture change at the hand-over is now an
accepted fault-only limitation (G2, §9.5). Recommendation: keep the receiver's disc mean, the one physically derived
value: a single constant cannot match every phase, and matching any one phase moves the others and leaves the
hand-over step essentially unchanged. No change is made; the mean-matching figures above are data. Stills at native size (fallback and refined, crescent, gibbous and below
the horizon at device pixel ratio 1 and 2, full Moon at ratio 1, plus labelled simulations of the 0.964 constant) are
kept with the owner's private evidence, not committed. Visual acceptability is the owner's judgement (G2).
