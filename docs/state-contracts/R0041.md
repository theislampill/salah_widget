## What this candidate implements so far

1. **Phase-direction authority** (`src/native/index.html` `nativeLunarSnapshot`).
   - Computes `phaseDirection` from the product ephemeris: +1 while α = acos(2f−1) falls over the next minute, −1
     while it rises, and 0 in a 1e−6 rad/min dead band.
   - It is distinct from `waxing` (the lit limb, χ sign), which is unchanged.
   - It is not produced for `simMoon` overrides or without a location. Those paths keep the historical waxing sign,
     so QA scenes are byte-unchanged.
   - Carried by `SalahMoonHost.capture` and by the head capture (`moon/src/moon-first-paint.mjs`).
   - Consumed by the leading edge (`moon/src/moon-precision.mjs`), which records `leadDirection` in the scene;
     by `phaseDrift` (`moon/src/moon-native.mjs`), signed by the record's own lead and unsigned when centred; and by
     both re-request skips, through `phaseDrift`.
   - Validated at capture admission.
2. **Presenter guarantee, same-scope expiry route** (`moon/src/moon-native.mjs` `poll`).
   - The expired record loses authority at once, but the presented detail and reveal classes are kept until the
     successor tier is adopted in the same task and a sky compose is requested.
   - Only when no successor can be adopted does the historical fallback run. That is the named residual R41-SWP-N.
   - Scope, epoch and clock changes keep their `clear()` withdrawal.
