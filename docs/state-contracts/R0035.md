## What the reconstruction changes

A disposable HTTP widget build receives additional hooks in the authored preview, refined publisher and existing native animation loop. The normal repository build continues to generate the shipped app. Both experimental tiers sample the same deterministic per-source function with the accepted host UTC. There is one preparation in flight, one replaceable pending scene and one in-flight time sample. A worker reconstructs each selected star's entire pixel-integrated point-spread function (PSF) from its actual fractional location, spectral source and extinction. A scalar changes that source's flux before the native calendar and painted-cloud operators and before display encoding. Overlapping stars contribute independently. Diffuse light, position, colour index and prayer calculations have no modulation term.

The refined eligibility input is the actual registered worker source and its complete detectable-contrast diagnostic. The bright preview uses its actual projected catalogue, physical occultation and displayed atmospheric background with the same contrast criterion. These stages can have different populations. The dark scene has 169 eligible preview sources and 1,404 eligible refined sources. Near the −18° solar altitude the counts are 134 and 1,406. Both tiers have zero eligible stars or changed pixels in the −4.79° twilight and daylight samples. The [original pre-host census](R0035-catalogue-scope.md) retains its own measurements and scope. [Reconstruction inputs and results](evidence/R0035/reconstruction.json)

A new publication without catalogue stars revokes its predecessor, including queued sampling and reported source counts. The equal-byte refined presentation path renews cloud authority only after checking the cloud receipt and current job. The worker independently projects the digest-verified catalogue at the accepted observer and view. It validates projected membership, duplicate IDs, coordinates and refined transport diagnostic membership before deriving PSFs from the catalogue records. On the dark qualification scene the complete 169-source detectable preview set equals the magnitude-selected refined set. Removing one real emitter from the independent native preview renderer reproduces the candidate's full three-channel patch to absolute linear error below 10⁻¹². The native lab requires exact agreement between the unmodulated reconstruction and the actual publisher's complete captured RGBA before admitting a result. A bounded set of potentially variable pixels comes from the sum of the complete positive PSF envelopes through the monotonic native foreground and encoder. Calendar material remains a different foreground owner. The lab discards incomplete, late or superseded results and withdraws its own overlay on a failure. A telemetry read returns a copy of the recorded state, including the current source IDs omitted by magnitude, under the glass, in its margin, or centered behind an opaque calendar or cloud pixel. The brightest sampled center is ranked by individual-source withdrawal after foreground and encoding. Its reported RGB delta includes neighbors at the same pixel. ## Physical and temporal limits

The unmeasured ×1 amplitude envelope is 0.04 × (1 − 0.75 sin altitude), corresponding to the small-aperture altitude weighting in the disabled CP9 surrogate. ×2, ×3 and ×4 scale that envelope. All four remain bounded by 0.16. Independent per-source, per-packet random seeds set the phases and nominal 0.6–4 Hz carriers. Two-second raised-cosine packets overlap on a separate stable time offset for each source. The 100 ms box exposure integrates the product of each sinusoid and the fade analytically. These choices determine this experiment's bounded zero-expectation signal. No finite observation is required to have an exactly zero sample mean.

Measured scintillation depends on sampling aperture, averaging time, zenith distance and turbulence. Dravins et al. report that lower stars often fluctuate more strongly and more slowly, with possible saturation. Their small-aperture variance exponent differs from the large-aperture cubic approximation. The reconstruction weights amplitude with altitude but retains the same frequency distribution at every altitude. The paper supplies no calibration from our parameters to a human observer looking through this card's glass. [Dravins et al., PASP 109 (1997), §§3.1, 8.1–8.2 and 10.2](https://adsabs.harvard.edu/pdf/1997PASP..109..173D)

A rejected perpetual-tone prototype repeated strongly at long lags. With the retained wave packets the maximum absolute autocorrelations were 0.075, 0.083 and 0.088 for the three brightest catalogue examples when sweeping every 0.05 s from 5 to 300 s, using 300 s of overlap at each lag. Across twelve stars in a separate 60 s ×2 synthetic sequence, the largest absolute mean fractional offset was 0.000051, the mean pair correlation was −0.00155 and the largest absolute pair correlation was 0.106. A second negative control revealed a collective pulse that signed pairwise correlations missed. Aligning packet boundaries across 640 equal-altitude stars produced a 1.397 maximum-to-minimum ensemble RMS ratio across eight phases. Staggering their boundaries by source ID reduced that ratio to 1.030. These are sample observations of the reconstruction. [Signal records](evidence/R0035/reconstruction.json)

## Which exclusions change the result?

The projected catalogue and physical lunar transit are independently testable. A source can stay inside the camera while the physical Moon removes its direct flux. HYG 27761 (V=4.56) stayed projected during a transit at 20:00 UTC on 23 December 2026. It had no transport diagnostic with its center 0.075° from the Moon. Ninety minutes to either side its transport diagnostic returned but the physical contrast gate still rejected it. Seven sidereal days later, at a similar position in the camera, it passed both gates. [Physical lunar occultation](evidence/R0035/physical-occultation.json)

A separate foreground test uses the real lunar WASM and pinned colour/terrain assets for four controlled calendar fractions. All four textured foregrounds kept every opaque-interior RGB byte unchanged over 60 samples each, with 62 eligible star centers inside the synthetic footprint. Each of the 240 frames changed exterior pixels. A three-zone cloud test produced zero changed pixels under opaque coverage and nonzero changes under partial coverage. The operator regression uses nonuniform cloud colours, lunar material, overlapping spectra, cropped edges and independent complete rerenders. Native placement, anti-aliasing and screenshot review on the app remain separate. [Lunar material](evidence/R0035/moon-material.json) · [Cloud and mask controls](evidence/R0035/reconstruction.json)

For a glass-boundary control we reused an earlier Chromium measurement of the left strap, buckle and six-row grid. We inferred individual row bounds from the recorded grid and spacing. The right strap's width was unavailable. This test uses bounding rectangles, with no browser backdrop filtering. At a single ×2 sample, before glass, we observed:

## Write cost and capture fidelity

Full and changed-rectangle arms calculate the same target bytes. The latter finds tight per-tile changed bounds, treats restoration of old pixels as a change, and forces full initialization after invalidation, resize or a failed partial upload. In 120 prescribed 60-Hz transitions over all eligible sources at ×1, 16 px tiles used median 188 calls and 2,098 pixels per update. With 64 px tiles the medians were 41 calls and 70,705 pixels. A complete upload uses one call and 172,250 pixels. Reducing the geometric work can still increase the number of browser calls. Neither measure estimates WebKit's frame-time tail. [Six tile sizes, both populations and ×1/×2](evidence/R0035/write-tradeoff.json)

The reconstruction's optional guard observes whole presentation intervals. Two consecutive 30-sample windows with p95 over 50 ms initiate a 60 s cooldown. Each dispatched sample changes the gain by at most 0.1 (at most 0.016 of nominal flux from the gain change at ×4). The test exercises suspension/retry and a guard-disabled control. Genuine wall-clock fade visibility, throughput and the cost of preparation, readback, structured cloning, worker transfer, layout reads and uploads still require measurements. A guard can suppress modulation while the card remains slow. A sustained browser slowdown, visible handoff gap or unreadable prayer content disqualifies that configuration.

Two lossless, uninterpolated 15 s sequences contain 900 independently calculated 325×530 frames each. Both use all eligible stars, no glass attenuation, the same fixed refined raster and exactly prescribed 60 Hz UTC samples. Every dirty-write reconstruction matched the full target and ten spaced frames matched the complete PSF oracle in each sequence. FFmpeg decoded each RGB clip to precisely the original 620,100,000 RGBA bytes. The saturated V=−1.44 star stayed white at its center as its wings varied. The V=4.53 star at (150,181) occupied three center-RGB states at ×1 and five at ×2. This material is synthetic-time, pre-glass evidence. It contains no native processing gaps, UI composition or human judgments. [Video, decoded-stream and frame hashes](evidence/R0035/sequence-receipt.json)

Run from a clean checkout using Node, Python, FFmpeg/ffprobe (libx264rgb) and Pillow for the optional crop sheet.

```sh
node --test tests/r0035-replay.test.cjs tests/r0035-star-scope.test.cjs
python tools/r0035/mutation_controls.py
node tools/r0035/run_replay.mjs /tmp/r35-reconstruction.json

python tools/r0035/sequence_crops.py /tmp/r35-sequences
python tools/r0035/materialize_lab.py /tmp/r35-widget
```

The last command copies the authored owners, adds scoped publication and diagnostic hooks there, runs the repository's normal builder and verifies V1. Its `r0035-lab-identity.json` records source head and byte identities. Use the experimental HTTP `index.html` entry. Its module worker and catalogue load local resources. The lab does not qualify the self-contained offline entry. One explicit comparison suffix is `&r35arm=changed&r35scope=all&r35amplitude=1&r35glass=0.5&r35margin=4`. The matched control changes only `r35arm=full`. Other experimental write tiles are 8/16/32/64/128/530 px, with 16 used unless specified. `r35guard=off` supplies a guard ablation. None of these flag values is a production selection.

The independent native driver accepts a JSON plan with `arms.main`, `arms.unmodified` and `arms.mitigated`. Each arm names a clean checkout `root`, full `commit` and optional producer `flags`. Each producer arm also gives a real QA predicate such as `"producerProbe":{"path":"stars.status","equals":"active"}`. Use the candidate's actual field name. Optional `probesByCell` entries such as `day/normal` or `dark/reduced` specify the correct inactive value. A plan with `"kind":"independent-reconstruction"` checks both experimental arms against the same lab receipt, identical inputs and distinct write policies. This is a different trial from one using the original candidate. Main must be a clean checkout in either case.

```sh
python tools/r0035/native_trials.py plan.json new-webkit-trials --engine webkit --rounds 9 --seconds 60
python tools/r0035/native_trials.py plan.json new-webkit-captures --engine webkit --rounds 1 --seconds 15 --capture
```

The driver uses loopback, fixed observer coordinates, synthetic weather and a local prayer response fixture. The default `--clock ordinary` lane shifts the wall date before the widget boots and leaves elapsed timers and rAF running normally. Admission checks the native wall-clock, advancing and preview flags, CSS fast mode, simulation-clock display and requested advancing UTC. The separately labeled `--clock simulation` lane uses the widget’s explicit 1× preview. Both lanes use fixtures. Trials validate a producer-only flag allowlist. Historical flags must have explicitly enumerated `historicalProducerOptions` in the plan and use the `r35` namespace.

Each measured trial samples scene and UI at its boundaries and approximately 1 Hz, recording the cost of each interior read. A gap over 1.25 s leaves scene coverage indeterminate. Gates include the solar range, selected calendar fraction and presentation, accepted painted cloud coverage and its receipt, card/header/buckle/footer and six populated prayer rows. Solar and phase controls use the physical and calendar owners respectively. The lab records each successfully presented evaluation, including a return to nominal and repeats of the same encoded pixels. Each receipt gives requested and validated UTC (one shared value), age, tier, scene identity, epoch, writes, whether the overlay contributed pixels, and whether its bytes changed. A center source ID and factor are available only when an encoded center contributes. A masked center can leave exposed PSF wings. A 13×13 control passed 17 changed wing pixels through the bridge with zero center alpha and unavailable center telemetry. Active-cell admission requires a maximum 100 ms gap between validated deliveries, including both interval boundaries, and advancing accepted sample timestamps. The pixel report independently records transitions against the previous successfully presented output in the same epoch, their maximum gap, and whether the overlay demonstrated encoded variation. Initial presentation and repeated codes supply no transition. A valid delivery interval with zero transitions retains its measured cadence and carries no demonstrated pixel variation or human visual verdict. The interval verdict records handoff gaps, guard suspension, missing history and overflow. Historical builds without suitable publication telemetry remain indeterminate. The shipped main arm measures baseline scene and card performance.

Raw rAF intervals, long tasks where supported, producer delivery and PNG acquisition times are reported separately. `--no-observe-producer` records timing with interior QA disabled and leaves scene qualification indeterminate. Keep engine, clock, observer, capture and motion lanes separate. PNG receipt times bound acquisition.
