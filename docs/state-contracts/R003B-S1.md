## 2. Part 1 — what was run (partial)

### 2.1 Method

`tools/qa/r003b_s1_capture.cjs` (global Playwright). The served tree is a `git archive` of `origin/main` `516d8cc8`,
the widget sits in a 325×530 iframe on a textured host page (the owner's embedding), the prayer record comes from
`tests/r0002-ordinary.json`, weather from an in-page clear-sky fixture, every other request is aborted, the clock is
mocked (`page.clock.install` then `resume`), location Central Florida (America/New_York). The refined owner is
re-invalidated every 400 ms for 7 s so the preview with "Sky preview · refining…" stays on screen, then released.

In the preview state and in the refined state, for every variant (test-only injected CSS; product source untouched)
one synchronised capture records: FNV hashes of both canvases before and after the composited screenshot (a capture
is `sync` only when nothing changed across it), PNG readbacks of both canvases, the iframe screenshot, producer state
(badge, refined status/draws/epoch/accepted, preview status/draws/utcMs, `real-sky-composed`/`-preview-ready`),
layer descriptors (backing, CSS rect, ratio, visibility, display, opacity, z-index, DOM order, `image-rendering`),
element boxes, DPR, WebGL renderer, user agent. A 100 ms time series spans the handoff, with up to 12 composited
samples. Variants: `base`, `nopreview` (preview canvas hidden), `nobackdrop` (every `backdrop-filter` off),
`onetoone` (the Part 2 CSS injected), `pixelated` (`image-rendering: pixelated`, diagnostic contrast),
`nobackdrop+onetoone`.

`tools/qa/r003b_s1_periodicity.py` extends `tools/qa/r0034_periodicity.py` (same residual: luminance − Gaussian σ 3,
4 px trimmed; same flat control: a fitted quadratic rounded to 8 bit) with per-axis 1-D spectra (pitch 2–16 px, `y` =
horizontal bands as in the owner's 90° peaks) on four crops: raw preview/refined canvas behind the rows (canvas px
100–300 × 380–500), composite sky-only (CSS 268–316 × 165–222), composite inside glass row 1 and row 2 (70–250 ×
294–320 / 329–355). Its `degrade` mode tests discriminator 7.

## 3. Part 2 — CI-02 1:1 candidate

### 3.1 Where the inset comes from

`.c` is 325×530 border-box with a 1 px border and `overflow:hidden`; its padding box is 323×528. Both sky canvases
(`.real-sky-preview`, `.real-sky-canvas`) are `position:absolute; inset:0; width:100%; height:100%` (generated
stylesheet authored in `tools/build_native.py`), so they fill the padding box, while their backing stores are 325×530
(`real-sky/native-preview-host.mjs`, `real-sky/native-host.mjs`). Ratio 1.00619 × 1.00379, `image-rendering: auto`.

### 3.2 Options

- **A — extend the canvases under the border**: `inset:-1px; width:calc(100% + 2px); height:calc(100% + 2px)` on
  the two canvas rules. 2 declarations in one authored file; producer, job dimensions, caches, encoders, worker,
  first-paint and every 325×530-pinned test untouched. The card's `overflow:hidden` still clips at the padding box,
  so the border, its radius, glass, shadow and every element box are unchanged; the outer 1 px ring of canvas pixels
  lies under the border.
- **B — backing 323×528**: the 325/530 literals occur about 150 times in the authored integration owners
  (`native-composition` 29, `native-host` 25, `native-star-preview` 22, `native-preview` 20, `native-preview-host`
  17, `native-worker` 52 generated from them, `src/native/index.html` 29), plus job dimensions, catalogue/regional
  caches, encoder byte-pinned tests and the foreground capture. It would change every preview/refined frame's pixels.

**Chosen: A** — far smaller, producer-neutral, and it keeps all 325×530-pinned contracts.

Implementation: `tools/build_native.py` (authored builder CSS) → regenerated `real-sky/native-sky.css`,
`offline.html` (inlined stylesheet) and `GENERATED.json`; `index.html` links the stylesheet and is byte-unchanged.
Build twice: clean. `python tools/verify_v1.py`: PASS.

Rect-based consumers adapt automatically (they read the canvas `getBoundingClientRect`): the Moon disc mask
(`nativeDiscMask`), the Moon bottom row, the foreground capture's blur scale (now 325/325), and the solar anchor in
`native-host-hooks.js`.

### 3.4 Residuals and limits of the candidate

- 1:1 holds at DPR 1 only; at other DPRs the 325 CSS px backing is still resampled to device pixels. Behaviour at
  DPR ≠ 1 is in §3.7 (a visible edge change in Chromium and WebKit at fractional DPR).
- Canvas content now maps to CSS by a −1 px offset instead of a ×323/325 scale: DOM overlays (arc, horizon line, sun
  disc, markers) stay put, so canvas features shift relative to them by up to 1 px at the edges and ~0.3 px near the
  horizon row; the outer 1 px of canvas pixels is hidden under the border.
- Baked clouds vs the hidden DOM cloud canvas: from the CSS alone a displacement of up to 1 px outward was expected
  (±0.5 px averaged over each half). Measured (§3.7), it is not observed: at most 0.06 px in both trees. Why the
  expected shift does not appear was not traced.
- The first-paint sky (`background-size:100% 100%` on the padding box, Moon host, `moon/src`) is still resampled, so
  the first-paint → preview handoff gains up to 1 px of displacement. Not measured.
- Corner sliver change (§3.3) — owner decision.
